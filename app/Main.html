Keep Scrolling

Reddit and X, minus the “get the app” nags.

Safari extension

What it does

Reddit’s blocking sheet, gone

Removes the “Get the app to keep using Reddit” bottom sheet and the fog overlay behind it.

X’s “Open X App” banner too

Clears the “See this post in the app” pop-up, the bottom banner and the top-bar pill, and stops Reply, Like and Repost taps from bouncing you to the App Store.

Scrolling actually works

Releases the scroll lock those nags leave on the page — hiding the sheet alone would leave it frozen.

Self-healing

The nags load late and come back. It keeps watching the page and clears them again, without a reload.

Nothing leaves your device

No account, no tracking, no servers. It runs only on reddit.com, x.com and twitter.com, and nowhere else.

Turn it on

  1. Open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions.
  2. Tap Keep Scrolling and switch it on.
  3. Set reddit.com, x.com and twitter.com to Allow Always, so Safari stops asking on every visit.

  2. Tick Keep Scrolling.
  3. Give it access to reddit.com, x.com and twitter.com on every website.

Then just browse. There is nothing to configure here — this screen is the whole app.
